# Contact Management App

## 1. Problems

### Too much contact information

Contact information is spread across different sources, which makes it difficult to manage and find the right contact.

### Missing history

Staff may not have a complete record of past interactions or commitments.

## 2. Goal

- Keep all contact information in one centralized location.
- Make it easy to search, filter, and manage contacts by geographical area and designation.

## 3. Feature requests

### Universal contact search

- Search and filter phone numbers and contacts by geographical area.
- Support searches by Assembly Constituency, Block/Tehsil, Gram Panchayat, Village, Panchayat Ward, Municipality, and Municipal Ward.

### Volunteer registration

- Allow people to register as volunteers directly through the application.

### Appointment and meeting management

- Schedule appointments and meetings.
- Attach relevant meeting documents.

### Area-wise statistics

- Display statistics based on geographical areas.
- Provide insights into contacts, volunteers, and other relevant data.

### Travel notifications

- Notify people in a particular area when the MLA plans to visit that area.

### Pin contacts

- Users can pin important contacts.

### Notes section

- Every contact has a notes and appointment section.

### Locals request for MLA (optional)

- Local people can request development or report issues in their area.

## 4. Geographical hierarchy

The application should organize contacts according to the following geographical structure.

- Lok Sabha Constituency
- Assembly Constituency
  - Rural areas
    - Block / Tehsil
    - Gram Panchayat
    - Village
    - Panchayat Ward
  - Urban areas
    - Municipality
    - Municipal Ward
    - Locality/Colony (optional)

**Note:** The geographical hierarchy should support both rural and urban areas. The application should allow users to find contacts at the appropriate administrative level.
